Daybook

Welcome to Daybook

A private journal that lives on this device.

  • Log as you go. Type what you just did and it is saved with the time.
  • Sum up the day. A few lines in Summary is enough.
  • Look back. The Journal tab has your calendar, search and weekly review.

Tip: in Safari, tap Share then Add to Home Screen to use it like an app.

    Back up and move

    Export a copy of your journal to keep, or bring pages in from elsewhere.

    Pages use the same layout as your project journal: one YYYY-MM-DD.md per day with Log, Notes, Conversations, Media and End of day.

    Receipts

    Photograph a receipt with the receipt button on the Today tab. It is saved in your journal and sent to Dext for your accountant.

    In Dext this is under your profile, and ends in @dext.cc. It is kept on this device, and with your account if you use sync.

    Privacy lock

    Ask for a passcode whenever Daybook opens.

    Daily reminder

    Add a phone reminder (Clock or Reminders app) that opens Daybook each evening. Home Screen apps on iPhone can't schedule their own alerts reliably.
